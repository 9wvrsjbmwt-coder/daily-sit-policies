Privacy Policy

Operator: BlackEdge Ventures, LLC Effective date: November 19, 2026 Contact: tyler@blackedgeventures.com

This policy describes the internal Daily Situation Report application (the “App”). The App helps authorized BlackEdge Ventures personnel view reports based on QuickBooks Online data from companies that authorize a connection.

Information the App uses

After a company authorizes the App, it reads accounting information needed for its reports, such as account balances, invoices, bills, payments, deposits, and related transaction details. The App also uses Microsoft sign-in information to restrict access and records operational logs needed to run and troubleshoot the service. Authorized users may provide report settings, monthly financial goals, and recipient addresses, and generate report files. If email is connected, the App also stores an encrypted Microsoft authorization token for the designated Outlook sender.

How the information is used

The App uses this information to create internal financial and operating reports, display previews, and provide downloads to authorized users. It does not write accounting transactions back to QuickBooks, sell QuickBooks data, or use it for advertising. When email delivery is enabled, an authorized sender may send reports as spreadsheet attachments with report visuals in the email body to recipient addresses saved for each company. These messages contain company financial information and are sent from a designated Outlook mailbox controlled by the App operator.

Service providers and access

Intuit supplies the QuickBooks connection. Microsoft Azure hosts the App, generated files, and operational logs, and Microsoft Entra provides sign-in. Microsoft Outlook and Microsoft Graph process report emails when delivery is enabled. A recipient’s email provider also processes any report sent to that recipient. Access to the App is restricted to personnel authorized by BlackEdge Ventures.

Storage and retention

The App stores QuickBooks connection credentials and report settings, monthly goals, recipient addresses, and, if connected, an encrypted Outlook authorization token in its Azure environment so authorized users do not have to reconnect or reenter settings for every report. Generated spreadsheet reports and cash outlook runs, including uploaded evidence used to create those runs, become eligible for deletion after 30 days. The App checks for expired files on its first request and then no more than once an hour while requests continue. If the App receives no requests, removal can occur later when it is used again. Settings and connection credentials remain until BlackEdge Ventures deletes them or the App is retired. Revoking access in QuickBooks prevents further data access but does not itself erase copies of existing reports or credentials stored in Azure. Sent emails may remain in sender and recipient mailboxes under their providers’ retention settings. Contact us to request deletion.

Security

The App uses HTTPS and Microsoft sign-in to limit access. BlackEdge Ventures restricts access to the Azure environment and stores QuickBooks connection credentials separately from the public source code. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Choices and questions

An authorized QuickBooks administrator may revoke the App’s access in QuickBooks. For questions about the App’s data use or requests concerning stored reports, contact tyler@blackedgeventures.com.

Changes

BlackEdge Ventures may update this policy when the App or its data handling changes. The current version will be posted at the public policy URL.
